\# Analysis 2 – Retention cohorts (casino)



\## Question

Do new casino players who arrive in different months come back at different rates?



\## Hypothesis (written before running any query, 7 Nov 2026)

Month-1 retention is stable across cohorts: every cohort's month-1 retention is

within 10 percentage points of the average over all cohorts.



\## Definitions

\- Population: new casino players (first seen on or after 1 May 2019).

\- Cohort: the month a player is first seen (Pacific time).

\- Month-k retention: share of the cohort that is active k months after the cohort month.

&#x20; Denominator = all players in the cohort.

\- Active = at least one approved deposit into gambling that month (decision 13).

\- Month-1 retention uses cohorts May 2019 – Jan 2020 (Feb 2020 has no month 1 in the data).

\- Unlike the funnel, a player can count as retained in month 2 without being active in month 1.



\## Result

| Cohort | Size | Month-1 retention (%) | Diff vs pooled avg (pts) |

|---|---|---|---|

| 2019-05 | 184 | 28.3 | +2.0 |

| 2019-06 | 140 | 34.3 | +8.1 |

| 2019-07 | 146 | 19.9 | −6.4 |

| 2019-08 | 127 | 24.4 | −1.8 |

| 2019-09 | 104 | 20.2 | −6.0 |

| 2019-10 | 115 | 26.1 | −0.1 |

| 2019-11 | 114 | 33.3 | +7.1 |

| 2019-12 | 139 | 28.8 | +2.6 |

| 2020-01 | 178 | 21.3 | −4.9 |



Pooled month-1 retention: 26.2%.



\- About one in four new casino players is active again in the month after arriving.

\- Retention falls fast in months 1–3, then flattens at about 10–15%: a small core of regular players.

\- With 100–180 players per cohort, differences of about ±6 points are within normal chance variation.



\## Confirmed or rejected?

Confirmed. All cohorts are within 10 points of the pooled average (−6.4 to +8.1).

Retention is a stable pattern, not a month-by-month effect.
