\# Analysis 1 – Deposit journey (casino)



\## Question

Where do new casino players drop off between first trying to fund their wallet

and becoming regular depositors?



\## Hypothesis (written before running any query, 3 Nov 2026)

The biggest drop-off is between the first and the second deposit into gambling.



\## Definitions

\- Population: new casino players (first seen on or after 1 May 2019),

&#x20; in cohorts May–Dec 2019, so every player has at least two full months of follow-up.

\- Funnel steps:

&#x20; 1. New player

&#x20; 2. Tried to fund the wallet

&#x20; 3. Funded successfully

&#x20; 4. First deposit into gambling

&#x20; 5. Second deposit into gambling

&#x20; 6. Active in month 1 after the cohort month

&#x20; 7. Active in month 2 after the cohort month

\- Active = at least one approved deposit into gambling that month (decision 13).

\- First withdrawal is reported separately: not every player should withdraw.



\## Result

| Step | Players | % of new players | % of previous step |

|---|---|---|---|

| New player | 1,069 | 100.0 | |

| Tried to fund | 1,065 | 99.6 | 99.6 |

| Funded successfully | 1,033 | 96.6 | 97.0 |

| First deposit into gambling | 1,028 | 96.2 | 99.5 |

| Second deposit | 566 | 52.9 | 55.1 |

| Active in month 1 | 288 | 26.9 | 50.9 |

| Still active in month 2 | 146 | 13.7 | 50.7 |



\- The path to the first deposit is nearly frictionless: 96% of new players reach it.

\- After the first deposit, about half of the players are lost at each step.

\- Only 14% of new players are still active two months after arriving.



\## Confirmed or rejected?

Partly confirmed.

\- In players lost, the first-to-second deposit step is the biggest drop (462 players, 43% of new players).

\- In share lost per step, the later steps lose slightly more (49% vs 45%).

\- Lesson: the hypothesis did not define "biggest". Later hypotheses state the measure explicitly.



\## Open questions

\- What is different about the 566 players who make a second deposit?

\- How quickly after the first deposit does the second one usually happen?
