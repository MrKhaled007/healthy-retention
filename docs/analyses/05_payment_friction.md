\# Analysis 5 – Payment friction (casino)



\## Question

Do declined payments push new casino players away?



\## Hypothesis (written before running any query, 7 Nov 2026)

New casino players whose first wallet funding attempt is declined have month-1

retention at least 5 percentage points lower than players whose first attempt is approved.



\## Definitions

\- Population: new casino players who tried to fund their wallet,

&#x20; cohorts May 2019 – Jan 2020 (month 1 is inside the data).

\- First funding attempt: the player's earliest wallet funding transaction

&#x20; (card or bank transfer), approved or declined.

\- Month-1 retention: active one month after the cohort month (decision 13).

\- Secondary view (not part of the hypothesis): players grouped by the number of

&#x20; declined funding attempts in the 30 days after their first attempt

&#x20; (0, 1, 2–5, 6+), with their month-1 retention.



\## Result



\### First funding attempt (hypothesis)

| First attempt | Players | Reached first deposit | Month-1 retention |

|---|---|---|---|

| Approved | 1,204 | 99.3% | 27.1% |

| Declined | 39 | 5.1% | 2.6% |



\### Declined funding attempts in the first 30 days (secondary)

| Declines | Players | % of players | Month-1 retention |

|---|---|---|---|

| 0 | 1,140 | 91.7 | 25.3% |

| 1 | 28 | 2.3 | 17.9% |

| 2–5 | 43 | 3.5 | 27.9% |

| 6+ | 32 | 2.6 | 68.8% |



\- A declined first attempt almost always ends the journey: 2 of 39 players ever deposited.

\- Many declines go with much higher activity: 68.8% month-1 retention for 6+ declines.

\- A decline means two different things depending on context.



\## Confirmed or rejected?

Confirmed: month-1 retention is 24.5 points lower after a declined first attempt

(threshold: 5 points). The group is small (39 players), but the gap is far beyond chance.



\## Recommendations

1\. Commercial: when a new player's first funding attempt is declined, guide them

&#x20;  immediately (retry advice, alternative payment method). Low risk, recovers blocked players.

2\. Duty of Care: treat 6+ declined funding
