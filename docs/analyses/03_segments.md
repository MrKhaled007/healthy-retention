\# Analysis 3 – Player segments (casino)



\## Question

What types of depositors exist, and how much of the deposit money comes from each type?



\## Hypothesis (written before running any query, 7 Nov 2026)

The high-intensity segment (top 10% of repeat depositors by monthly deposit amount)

accounts for more than 50% of all approved deposit money from casino players.



\## Definitions

\- Population: all casino players with at least one approved deposit into gambling

&#x20; (new and existing players).

\- Monthly deposit amount = total approved deposits into gambling ÷ months observed

&#x20; (from the month of the first deposit to Feb 2020, inclusive).

\- Segments:

&#x20; - One-and-done: exactly 1 approved deposit

&#x20; - Light: 2+ deposits, monthly deposit amount below the median of repeat depositors

&#x20; - Regular: 2+ deposits, from the median up to the 90th percentile

&#x20; - High-intensity: 2+ deposits, above the 90th percentile

\- "High-intensity" is a spending proxy, not confirmed gambling harm.



\## Result

Thresholds (repeat depositors): median $77.14 per month, 90th percentile $1,038.33 per month.



| Segment | Players | % of players | Deposit money | % of money | Median $/month | Deposit days/month | Avg funding declines |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| One-and-done | 847 | 37.1 | $89,770 | 1.5 | 9 | 0.3 | 0.0 |

| Light | 719 | 31.5 | $172,662 | 2.9 | 22 | 0.6 | 0.2 |

| Regular | 576 | 25.2 | $1,752,048 | 29.7 | 250 | 2.9 | 2.0 |

| High-intensity | 144 | 6.3 | $3,881,316 | 65.8 | 1,941 | 7.1 | 27.0 |



\- 6.3% of depositors provide 65.8% of deposit money.

\- 68.6% of depositors (one-and-done + light) provide 4.4% of deposit money.

\- High-intensity players average 27 declined funding attempts, versus 2 for regular players.

\- Total = 2,286 players, matching the published casino customer count.



\## Confirmed or rejected?

Confirmed: the high-intensity segment provides 65.8% of deposit money (threshold: 50%).
